WHY OVERWATCH EXISTS

Better information should strengthen human command.

Operational teams often have fragments of the picture spread across radios, messaging, maps, spreadsheets, calls and individual experience. Overwatch exists to bring those fragments together without pretending software owns the judgement.

01

Human first. Human last.

AI and automation can organise information and recommend work. Material operational, safeguarding, clinical, legal, financial and safety authority remains with people.

02

Evidence before claims.

We distinguish live, verified capability from prototypes, concepts and planned integrations. A polished screen is not evidence that an integration is operational.

03

Useful before complicated.

The controller should be able to understand the situation and act quickly. Intelligence can be sophisticated behind the workflow without making the front end difficult.

PART OF ORVIA

Separate enough to sell clearly. Connected enough to work properly.

Overwatch has its own product site and commercial story because command, tracking and communications are a distinct buyer problem. Underneath, it connects to the same wider ORVIA architecture: VERA/Hive, IRIS, ARIA, Voice, governed documents and the sales/operational workflow.

  • ORVIA Oversight remains the parent organisation.
  • ORVIA Voice remains a separate communications product with cross-links.
  • Overwatch houses Command, Track and PTT as an operational platform family.
  • Customer information and follow-up should join the common ORVIA sales process.

DESIGN CONTRIBUTION

Built from practical operational thinking.

Darren is credited for operational workflow design contribution. ORVIA’s implementation is sector-neutral and is being developed as its own governed platform rather than reproducing an organisation-specific external application.

The design rule

Keep the essential loop visible: situation → objectives → people and roles → resources → authorised tasking → decisions → updates → recovery → review.

Judge it against a real operational problem.
