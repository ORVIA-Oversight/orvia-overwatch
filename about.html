About Overwatch

Teams usually have fragments of the picture

One system has the radio. Another has location. Another has the incident report. Control is left to mentally assemble the situation. Overwatch exists to bring those signals together — without taking the decision away from the people who should be making it.

What we believe

The principle the whole platform is built on

Humans stay in command

Overwatch provides visibility, communication and a record. It does not make command, clinical or safety decisions — your people do, always.

Visibility, not surveillance

Location and status tracking is scoped to operational need. We're building toward clear governance around what's tracked, when, and why.

Say what's real

We label concept work as concept work, and we don't claim a capability is live until it's been verified end-to-end. That applies to this website as much as anywhere else.
