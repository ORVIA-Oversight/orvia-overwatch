ONE LOOP

From first report to accountable close-out.

The simple controller workflow sits at the front. ORVIA intelligence and data services sit behind it.

  • Capture the issue and establish the current situation.
  • Set objectives, roles, sectors and actions.
  • See suitable resources and their operational status.
  • Review recommendations before a human authorises tasking.
  • Maintain a timestamped decision and event record through recovery and review.
