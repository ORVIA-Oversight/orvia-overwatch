Why Overwatch

Most teams have fragments of the picture

One system has the radio. Another has location. Another has the incident report. Control is left to assemble the situation in their head.

SEE

Where is everyone?

Personnel, teams and resources on one live map — positions, status and task, in one place instead of scattered across tools.

TALK

What are they saying?

Cross-device push-to-talk with talkgroups for command, operations, medical and logistics — reachable from a phone, tablet or browser.

CONTROL

What's happening, and what was decided?

Incidents, resources and decisions in one view, with a timestamped record of what was decided and by whom.
