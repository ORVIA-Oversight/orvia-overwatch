01 · ORVIA COMMAND

The controller surface for the whole operation.

Command is designed to answer the questions a control room actually needs to answer: What is happening? What matters most? Who owns it? What resources are available? What has been decided? What is still unresolved?

It can support multiple concurrent incidents or workstreams while keeping human authority visible. The platform can suggest, organise and surface information; the controller decides what gets tasked.

FROM FIRST REPORT TO ACCOUNTABLE CLOSE-OUT

A simple front end with human control at the important points.

Overwatch is intended to reduce fragmentation, not add another layer of complexity. The controller gets a current picture; the system keeps the evidence and status underneath it.

01

Receive

A report, call, operator update or field message enters the operational picture.

02

Understand

Build the current situation, expose gaps and identify what must be verified.

03

Decide

The human controller sets priorities, objectives and operating intent.

04

Task

Resources are reviewed and explicitly authorised for consequential allocation.

05

Record + review

Updates, outcomes and decisions remain visible for recovery, governance and learning.

Human authority boundary: recommendation is not dispatch. A nearest-resource suggestion does not automatically mean send that resource. Capability, readiness, residual coverage, geography, current tasking and local rules still matter.

HOW THE ORVIA SYSTEM JOINS

Operational simplicity at the front. Governed truth underneath.

Overwatch is part of the wider ORVIA architecture. The design direction is for each layer to do one clear job rather than making one interface pretend to be everything.

ARIAVoice and access layer. Receives or supports communications where configured.
→
IRISConductor/intelligence layer. Organises context, gaps and recommended next steps.
→
COMMANDHuman operational control surface for priorities, objectives, tasks and decisions.
→
VERA / HIVEGoverned record layer for canonical incidents, resources, actions and status history.
Evidence-first status rule: the diagram explains the architecture and direction of travel. It does not mean every integration is automatically enabled for every deployment. Customer-specific connections are only represented as live once configured and verified.
