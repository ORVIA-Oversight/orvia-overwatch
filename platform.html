Platform

Three parts that were built to work as one

Most organisations already have some of this — a tracking app here, a radio app there, an incident spreadsheet somewhere else. Overwatch brings them together.

01 — ORVIA Track

Know where your teams and resources are

A live map of personnel, teams, vehicles and resources — positions, status and task, updated as things move. Set objectives, routes, geo-fenced areas and rendezvous points so everyone is working from the same picture.

02 — ORVIA PTT

Cross-device push-to-talk, not another walkie-talkie app

Talk across phones, tablets and browser-based control-room seats over configurable talkgroups — Command, Operations, Medical, Logistics, or groups you define. Designed to work alongside existing radios, not force you to replace them overnight.

03 — ORVIA Control

Incidents, resources and decisions, together

A command view of active incidents, available and tasked resources, and a timestamped decision log — who decided what, and when. Built to support incident commanders and controllers, not replace their judgement.

Connectivity

Works on the connectivity you actually have

Overwatch depends on suitable connectivity — Wi-Fi, mobile data, or a private network. We're not going to tell you it works with no signal at all; where connectivity is limited, the interface shows that clearly rather than guessing.

Web-based

No app installation required for control-room or browser-based use.

Cross-device

Phones, tablets, laptops and control-room displays — not tied to one hardware vendor.

Visible status

Live, delayed and offline states are shown honestly, not hidden behind a green light.
