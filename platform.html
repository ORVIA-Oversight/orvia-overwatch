MODULAR BY DESIGN

One platform. Several operational layers.

Use only the modules an operation needs, or join them into a common operating picture. The architecture is designed around governed data, human authority and visible evidence.

01

ORVIA Command

Command is the controller surface: incident or issue, current situation, objectives, roles, sectors, actions, resources, human-authorised allocation, decision log, stabilisation, recovery and review.

Multiple concurrent incidents
Structured situation reports
Roles, sectors and objectives
Actions, owners and priority
Resource status and allocation
Timestamped event and decision log

02

ORVIA Track

The geospatial layer for teams, vehicles, assets, hazards, zones, rendezvous points and tasking. Track is designed to feed Command with location-aware operational context while remaining usable in the field.

Common operational map
Field positions and status
Zones, hazards and objectives
ATAK-compatible direction of travel

03

ORVIA PTT

Operational voice across field and control environments, with talkgroups and future transcription/record linkage where configured. It is intended to work with existing communications rather than forcing a single hardware estate.

Field + control-room use
Configurable talkgroups
Cross-platform direction
Communications linked to operational context

04

IRIS + ARIA

IRIS is the intelligence/conductor layer and ARIA is the voice/access layer. They can prepare information, identify gaps, assist with communications and recommend next steps. Consequential decisions remain human-authorised.

Missing-information prompts
Situational synthesis
Resource recommendations
Voice and communications assistance

05

VERA / Hive

The governed data layer intended to hold canonical incidents, resources, actions, roles, sectors, decisions, communications and status history. Overwatch should show what is known, what is provisional and where it came from.

HOW IT JOINS

Simple at the front. Governed underneath.

1

Receive

Report, call, sensor, operator or field update.

2

Understand

Build the current situation and expose gaps.

3

Decide

Human controller sets priorities and objectives.

4

Task

Resources are reviewed and explicitly authorised.

5

Record

Updates, decisions, outcomes and review are preserved.

INTEGRATION DIRECTION

Designed to connect, not become another silo.

Overwatch is being joined to the wider ORVIA environment: Voice for communications, Hive/VERA for truth and persistence, IRIS for intelligence, SharePoint for governed files, and the existing commercial pipeline for enquiries and follow-up.

Human authority boundary

Recommendation is not dispatch.

A nearest-resource suggestion does not automatically mean send that resource. Capability, readiness, current tasking, geography, residual coverage and local rules all matter. The controller authorises consequential tasking.
