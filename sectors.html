Sectors

What kind of operation do you run?

The underlying platform is the same everywhere — what changes is how it's set up. Here's what Overwatch is designed for across different operational environments.

MILSIM & AIRSOFT

Command a large-scale exercise properly

Live team and squad positions, objectives, marshal and medical locations, and event-wide comms — instead of running a 200-player event on radio calls and guesswork.

EVENTS

One picture for event control

Security, medical, stewards, logistics and production working from the same map and the same incident log — with temporary access for staff who only need it for the day.

FIRE & RESCUE

Sector awareness across an active incident

Crew and appliance positions by sector, objectives, and a live resource count — built for teams who already know how to run an incident, not to replace their command structure.

SEARCH & RESCUE

Team positions across difficult terrain

Live positions and comms where mobile signal is patchy and weather changes fast — with visible offline/degraded status rather than false confidence in a connection that's dropped.

EMERGENCY RESPONSE

Coordinate a fast-moving, multi-team response

A shared operational picture across responder teams working a live, changing situation — without implying any formal emergency-service accreditation we don't hold.

SECURITY & REMOTE TEAMS

Visibility without invasive surveillance

Know where mobile officers and remote teams are and when something changes — scoped to operational need, not constant tracking outside working periods.

A note on evidence

What we will and won't claim

The sectors above describe what Overwatch is designed and being built for. We won't claim formal adoption by any emergency service, defence organisation, or government body unless that's genuinely true and evidenced — and the same goes for specific reliability, coverage or response-time figures. If you're evaluating Overwatch for your sector, ask us directly what's live today versus in development; we'd rather tell you than have you find out the hard way.
