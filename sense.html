THE DEVELOPMENT MODEL

SENSE → EDGE → TRACK → COMMAND.

The proposed architecture keeps high-rate sensing close to the event, turns raw inputs into controlled operational state, and only presents each authorised user with information appropriate to their role.

01

SENSE

Radar, UWB, GPS and other approved sources create observations with source, confidence and age.

02

EDGE

On-site processing normalises, buffers and fuses observations so the local event can continue during WAN loss.

03

TRACK

Locations, contacts, objectives and operational state are brought into one mapped picture without hiding uncertainty.

04

COMMAND

Human controllers, commanders and authorised users receive role-specific information and retain decision authority.

TRUTH BEFORE CERTAINTY

A detection is not automatically an identity.

SENSE is being designed around explicit evidence states. A sensor may detect movement without knowing who or what caused it. The system should preserve that uncertainty rather than converting it into a confident label.

One field. Different signals.

RADARAnonymous presence and movement inside defined sensing zones.
UWBIssued tag identity and accurate local positioning where deployed.
GPS / GNSSBroader outdoor positioning from authorised participant devices.

DESIGNED FOR FAILURE

The field should not disappear when the internet does.

The development direction is local-first. Sensing, role filtering and the event picture should continue on site while cloud synchronisation queues for recovery.

LOCAL

Operational continuity

Local services are intended to continue processing sensor and participant state when remote connectivity is unavailable.

BACKHAUL

Multiple WAN options

Starlink, 4G/5G or venue internet can provide uplink. None should become the sole operating dependency.

SYNC

Reconnect without reset

Event records should be buffered locally and reconciled once connectivity returns rather than losing the operational trail.
